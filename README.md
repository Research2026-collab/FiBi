# FiBi – Digital Planning Tools for Financial Education

Copyright © 2026 Elisabeth Vanderheiden.

FiBi is a collection of digital tools for adult educators and programme planners supporting target group-oriented planning, professional development and the transfer of approaches to financial education into educational practice.

## Overview

The repository currently brings together four interconnected components:

* **Persona.Werkstatt** – an interactive planning tool based on realistic learner personas
* **Digitale Kurzfortbildung** – a short digital professional development module on persona-based and target group-oriented programme planning
* **Angebot to go** – a tool for developing and documenting an educational offer
* **Feedback** – a low-threshold feedback instrument accompanying the practical use and further development of the three applications

## Persona.Werkstatt

The FiBi Persona.Werkstatt supports the development of educational offers by combining user-centred planning with structured information about typical participants in adult education.

The application provides 15 personas representing diverse life situations, educational experiences and financial circumstances. These include, for example, an apprentice, a family caregiver, a retired person, a single parent, a migrant learner and a civically engaged adult.

Each persona contains information on

* life situation
* educational background
* financial circumstances
* learning interests
* barriers to participation
* preferred learning conditions

Users select one persona and enter key information about a planned educational activity, including

* topic
* objectives
* format
* duration
* organisational framework

Based on these inputs, the application generates

* a draft course description suitable for programme announcements
* an internal planning document summarising how the selected persona’s needs, interests and barriers have been considered

The planning document can be transferred directly into the accompanying FiBi Material Pool.

## Digitale Kurzfortbildung

The digital short professional development module introduces the use of personas for target group-oriented programme planning. It supports practitioners in examining planning decisions in relation to different life situations, educational experiences, resources and barriers to participation and connects these considerations with the practical use of the Persona.Werkstatt.

## Angebot to go

Angebot to go supports the development of an educational offer in a compact digital format. It complements the Persona.Werkstatt by providing an additional route for translating planning considerations into a concrete educational offer.

## Feedback

The integrated feedback instrument accompanies the practical use and further development of the digital tools. Users can provide feedback on the Persona.Werkstatt, the digital professional development module and Angebot to go.

The feedback instrument records the educational field and context of use, ratings of comprehensibility, usefulness and transferability, and optional open comments on helpful aspects, difficulties, missing elements and suggestions for further development. Name, institution and e-mail address are voluntary.

## Purpose

FiBi supports programme planning in financial education that takes different educational biographies, financial situations and barriers to participation into account. The tools connect target group-oriented planning with professional development, practical application and structured feedback from educational practice.
